The Honda CG 125 is a popular commuter motorcycle renowned for its reliability, affordability, and ease of maintenance, particularly in regions like Pakistan where it's widely produced and used; powered by a simple, air-cooled, 125cc, single-cylinder engine with overhead valves, the CG 125 offers excellent fuel efficiency, making it ideal for daily commutes and long distances, while its sturdy frame and design ensure stability on various terrains; with readily available spare parts and widespread mechanic knowledge, the CG 125 has remained a staple choice for riders seeking a practical and dependable motorcycle with a long history dating back to the 1970s
